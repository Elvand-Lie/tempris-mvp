TEMPRIS COLLECTOR — NMAP AND NPCAP EXTERNAL PREREQUISITE NOTICE

1. External Prerequisite Boundary:
   Nmap and Npcap are external operator/customer prerequisites only.
   Tempris does NOT bundle, host, download, provision, update, redistribute,
   or accept Nmap or Npcap as signed managed-manifest or offline artifact components.

2. Licensing and Distribution Policy:
   Tempris does NOT hold an OEM redistribution license for Nmap or Npcap.
   Consequently, no Nmap or Npcap binary, installer, or driver is bundled in
   any Tempris package, hosted update repository, or release artifact.

3. Customer / Operator Responsibility:
   Customers and system operators who wish to use network discovery or port scanning
   features must obtain, license, and install official copies of Nmap and Npcap directly
   from their official distributors (https://nmap.org and https://npcap.com).

4. Tempris Integration:
   Tempris Collector only discovers and validates existing, locally installed
   Nmap and Npcap installations on the host. Tempris will never automatically
   download, install, or update Nmap or Npcap.
