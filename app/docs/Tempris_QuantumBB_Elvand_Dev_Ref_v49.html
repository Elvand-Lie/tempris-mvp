08 · TACF — Tempris AI Control Framework · v1.0 · Wave 1 Implementation
AI Control Framework — build this into every AI-powered feature
TACF v1.0 is the governance model for all autonomous and AI-assisted actions within the Tempris platform. Every AI-powered feature must implement TACF. Accountability always remains with a human authority layer — never the AI. All AI actions must be traceable, auditable, and reversible.
TACF Core Principle: Tempris defines what can be done (permission layer). The client defines what should be done (objective layer). The AI executes within those boundaries (execution layer). The AI never has authority — only the human operator does. Every AI action is logged to AuditLog with full trace: model, prompt hash, input, output, operator_id, timestamp.
STEP 1
REQUEST
Client defines what should be done — objective, scope, risk appetite
STEP 2
DECISION
Policy engine validates against TACF constraints — is this permitted?
STEP 3
CONTROL
Tempris defines what can be done — permission framework, blast radius cap
STEP 4
EXECUTION
AI executes within approved boundaries only — traceable, reversible
STEP 5
AUDIT
Every action committed to AuditLog — Wave 3: on-chain SEALCHAIN proof
TACF — 6 CONTROL DOMAINS · ALL MUST BE IMPLEMENTED IN WAVE 1
TACF-D1 · GOVERNANCE
AI Governance & Accountability
Establishes who owns what. Defines accountability chains and oversight obligations for all autonomous AI actions.
  • D1.1 AI Accountability Register — every AI feature listed with owner, purpose, risk level
  • D1.2 Decision Rights Matrix — what can AI decide autonomously vs what needs human approval
  • D1.3 Human Override Protocol — any AI action can be stopped/reversed by authorised user
  • D1.4 Founder/board AI risk disclosure — quarterly review of AI feature risk register
TACF-D2 · POLICY ENGINE
Permission & Constraint Framework
Technical and contractual ruleset governing what any AI agent is permitted to do. Enforced at runtime, not just by policy document.
  • D2.1 Execution boundary definition — per module, per AI feature, per tenant
  • D2.2 Approval threshold tables — actions requiring human sign-off before execution
  • D2.3 Blast radius caps — limits on how many assets/systems one AI action can touch
  • D2.4 Rate limiting & circuit breakers — AI cannot spam APIs or flood systems
TACF-D3 · RISK CONTROL
Agent Risk Management
Continuous assessment of AI behaviour against expected baselines. Rollback capability and real-time kill-switch authority.
  • D3.1 Behavioural baseline monitoring — flag anomalous AI output patterns
  • D3.2 Autonomous action risk scoring — every AI-recommended action gets a risk score before execution
  • D3.3 Rollback & reversal capability — all AI-driven changes must be reversible where possible
  • D3.4 Emergency kill-switch — global AI pause, per-module AI pause, per-tenant AI pause
TACF-D4 · TRANSPARENCY
Explainability & Traceability
Every AI action traceable to a specific permission, request, and outcome. No black-box decisions in production.
  • D4.1 Decision explanation logs — every AI recommendation includes "why" in plain language
  • D4.2 Action → outcome tracing — full chain: input → model → output → action → result
  • D4.3 AuditLog entry for every AI call — model used, prompt hash, token count, operator_id
  • D4.4 Regulator-ready exports — AI action log exportable for MAS/CSA review
TACF-D5 · COMPLIANCE
Regulatory Alignment
Maps TACF controls to MAS TRM, PDPA, CSA, ISO 42001, EU AI Act, and NIST AI RMF.
  • D5.1 MAS TRM AI Annex — explainability and human oversight requirements met
  • D5.2 PDPA automated decision controls — no automated decisions affecting individuals without human review
  • D5.3 ISO 42001 AI Management System — documented AI governance process
  • D5.4 EU AI Act risk category — classify all AI features by risk tier (limited/high/unacceptable)
TACF-D6 · ASSURANCE
Audit & Continuous Assurance
Immutable audit trail. Every TACF control check, every AI action, every override is committed to AuditLog (stub in Wave 1 → SEALCHAIN in Wave 3).
  • D6.1 Immutable audit log — AuditLog table, append-only, no deletes permitted
  • D6.2 Tamper detection — periodic hash chain verification of AuditLog integrity
  • D6.3 Timestamp integrity — UTC timestamps, NTP-synced, logged with entry
  • D6.4 Wave 3 upgrade path — AuditLog designed to feed SEALCHAIN without schema migration
