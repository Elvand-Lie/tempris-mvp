## Scope arithmetic

Distinct findings and affected-asset occurrences are intentionally different. If `F-1` is confirmed on `A-1`, `A-2`, and `A-3`, the platform reports one confirmed finding, three confirmed links, and up to three affected assets. Tenant TES includes `F-1` once.

Reference, not-applicable, resolved, suggested, and legacy-unverified counts are mutually meaningful lifecycle views but are not all mathematically disjoint in the raw legacy table. The canonical service defines the output categories and exposes the `scope_version` so snapshots remain comparable.
